## Welcome to Your SaaS Starter

This is a sample blog post. Replace it with your own content.

### What is included

This starter kit comes with everything you need to launch a production-ready SaaS application:

- **Authentication** - Email/password login with NextAuth
- **Payments** - Stripe integration with checkout, webhooks, and customer portal
- **Blog** - Markdown-based blog engine with frontmatter support
- **Free Tools** - Public interactive tools to drive signups
- **Dark Theme** - Premium glassmorphism design
- **Mobile First** - Responsive design with bottom nav on mobile

### Next steps

1. Update your branding in `src/app/layout.tsx`
2. Add your data models to `prisma/schema.prisma`
3. Build your API routes and pages
4. Configure Stripe for payments
5. Deploy to production

Happy building!
